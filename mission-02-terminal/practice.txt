### Task 2.2 — Safety Practice Questions
* **Difference between replacing and adding text:** Replacing overwrites everything inside the file from scratch, while adding attaches the new text to the end of what was already there.
* **Difference between copying and moving:** Copying leaves the original file alone and builds a twin. Moving transfers the file to a completely new home or gives it a new name.
* **What current-directory display provides:** It acts like a digital map coordinate showing your precise folder location.
* **Why directory listing is useful:** It lets you see all your files instantly without clicking around visually.
